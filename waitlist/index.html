Hey, I'm Lauren

After having my second child in April 2026, things went downhill for me, and in August I was diagnosed with severe postpartum depression. Some days I couldn't even tell you how I felt, let alone write it down in a cute journal. So I built the thing I needed: a journal on my phone that does a lot of the work for you when you don't have the words.

It's not therapy and it's not a fix. It's my experience turned into something you can actually use on the hard days.

In November, I posted a video of my mom sitting on the bathroom floor with me, brushing out my hair because I couldn't do it on my own.

It was seen 15.8 million times.Moms left over 12,000 comments.

Here's what some of them said:

“I thought I was the only mama that silently went through these moments.”

“...that was me sat on the floor but with nobody behind me...”

“I don't know how I survived the first year of being a mom. Everyday was a struggle like I've never experienced before...”

“...to even see that I was struggling so badly”

“Man its tough in the trenches alone.”

That's who I made this for.
