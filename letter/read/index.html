A letter for you

From my floor to yours.

Hey friend,

If you're reading this, you probably found me through the hair video. So hi. I'm really glad you're here.

If you're in it right now, I'm so sorry. I know how heavy it feels, and how lonely it can get even in a house full of people. You're not crazy and you're not doing it wrong. You're tired and you're hurting, and that's a lot to carry.

Here's my honest version. I was diagnosed with severe postpartum depression, and I'm still in it, still finding my way out. It's hard to explain how something as simple as brushing your hair can feel like too much. Mine had gotten so tangled that I couldn't even face it. So my mom sat on the bathroom floor with me and brushed out every single knot.

I have a village that carries me, and I know how lucky I am. But I read your comments. So many of you said you never had anybody sitting behind you. Some of you said you went through it quietly and nobody even noticed.

I read them all, and I sat in it for a while. I want to be that person for you. I don't want anyone going through this alone.

Every week

I'm going to send you an email. Nothing fancy, just me checking on you. Some weeks it'll be something that helped me. Some weeks it'll be a mess of a story from my house. Some weeks it'll just be a reminder that you're doing better than you think.

Think of it as me sitting on the floor with you.

Having help doesn't mean you can't struggle. Struggling doesn't make you a bad mom. Both of those took me way too long to believe, so I'll keep reminding you until you do.

Be gentle with yourself this week. If all you did today was keep everybody alive, that counts.

I'm not a doctor or a therapist, just a mom who's been there and gets it.

I'm right here with you,
Lauren

If it ever gets really dark, please don't wait. Call or text 988, or the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-852-6262 (free, 24/7). In immediate danger, call 911.
