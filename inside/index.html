Good to know

A few things that make it different.

You can't get behind

There are no streaks and no grades. Skip a day, a week, or a question. One word counts.

It fills your therapist list for you

Under most answers there's a button to add it to your summary. Ratings and hard moments go on by themselves.

It checks back in

Some pages ask you to answer again in a few weeks, then show you what changed.

Your recovery key

If you forget your password, it's the only way back into your pages, because nobody else can open them.
