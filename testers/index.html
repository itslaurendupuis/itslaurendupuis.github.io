Set it up

Tap each circle when you finish a step. It only saves on your phone.

Open the journal on your phone

Use Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android. Not inside Instagram or Facebook, because the home screen step won't work there.

finding-me-again.web.appOpen it

Make your account

Tap New here? Make your account. Use an email you check. The screen says to use the email you bought it with. You didn't buy it, so any email is fine.

Pick a password you'll remember. A short phrase works great. Your password also locks your pages.

Save your recovery key

You'll see a code like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Screenshot it or write it down. Then tick I saved my recovery key and tap Open my journal.

Please don't skip this one. If you forget your password, this key is the only way back into your pages. I can't reset it for you, and that's on purpose. It's what keeps your pages private.

Pick your colors

Choose whatever feels good to open. You can change it anytime at the bottom of any screen. There's also a Bigger text button there.

Put it on your home screen

Then it opens with one tap, like an app. No app store. About 30 seconds.

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button, the square with the arrow up.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Look for the heart.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the three dots in the top corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Add. Look for the heart.

The same steps are inside the journal too, under Get set up on Today.

Optional: fill in Get set up

On Today there's a Get set up card: your people, doctor numbers, medications and a few more. Do them whenever. They make the hard-day tools work better.

Where to next? At the bottom of each section, buttons to the other three.

Your week (a loose plan)

If you like a plan, here's one. If you don't, ignore it and wander. Both help me. Missed a day? Just pick up wherever.

How to send me notes

Notes go straight to me from inside the journal. No forms, no email.

Anytime: a quick note

Tap Send Lauren a note on the card at the top of Today, or Testing? Send Lauren a note about this screen at the bottom of any screen. It remembers which screen you were on, so you don't have to explain where you were.

Your privacy

Plain version: I can't read your journal.

Stays private

  • Everything you write in the journal
  • Your check-ins, ratings and notes to yourself
  • Your people and medications lists

Your pages are scrambled on your own phone before they're saved. Nobody else can read them. Not me, not Google, who runs the servers.

Comes to me

  • Only what you type on the Notes for Lauren page
  • The first name you type there, if you type one
  • Which screen your note was about

I'll use your notes to make the journal better. I'll only share your words if you say yes in the end-of-week questions.

This isn't medical care

It's a journal made by a mom who's been there. It's not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a replacement for your doctor or therapist.

Questions

I forgot my password⌄

On the login screen, tap Forgot your password? You'll get an email to set a new one. The first time you open your journal after that, it asks for your recovery key once. That's how your pages stay locked to you.

I lost my recovery key⌄

If you still know your password, you're fine. Just keep logging in. If you lose both, your old pages can't be opened, by anyone. Tell me and we'll get you a fresh start.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?⌄

Yes. Log in with the same email and password and your pages are there.

Does it cost anything?⌄

Not for you. You're testing it, so it's free.

What happens to my account after the week?⌄

Keep using it if you want. If anything changes, I'll tell you first.

Something looks old, or a change isn't showing⌄

I'll be fixing things during the week. The journal only picks up changes when it starts fresh. If you opened it from your home screen, swipe it away in your app switcher, then open it again. In Safari or Chrome, pull down on the page to refresh. It'll ask for your password again, because it locks itself when it restarts.

Something looks broken. Is it me?⌄

It's not you. That's exactly what this week is for. Send me a note from that screen and tell me what phone you have.

Thank you. Really.
